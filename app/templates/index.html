American Sign Language Fingerspelling Alphabet, Control Signs & Word Formation

How do Signs Form Words and Sentences?
1. Fingerspelling (A–Z): Form hand gestures one by one to spell words (e.g. H → E → L → P).
2. Hold-to-Commit (~0.5s): Hold each sign steady until the green progress meter reaches 100% to commit the letter.
3. Predictive Autocomplete: Click suggested word chips (e.g. HELLO, WATER) to complete words instantly.
4. Control Signs: Make the Space sign to finish a word and hear TTS audio; make Delete to backspace.
